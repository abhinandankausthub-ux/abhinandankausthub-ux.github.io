🧠 About

I hold an MS in Aerospace Engineering from Auburn University (2026), specializing in structural integrity, fatigue & fracture mechanics, and composite materials. My work focuses on predicting material failure, crack propagation, and reliability of engineering systems under realistic loading conditions.

I build my own engineering tools: fatigue crack growth with the Paris, Walker, and Forman models, and composite laminate analysis with Classical Lamination Theory (CLT). I check them against published worked examples, because a tool you can't validate is just a pretty plot. 🙂

My technical experience spans finite element analysis, fatigue life prediction, fracture mechanics, and composite failure modeling, with hands-on experience in Python, ANSYS, and engineering simulation workflows.

I'm based in Hyderabad and looking for opportunities where I can apply structural mechanics to high-impact engineering problems in aerospace, energy, and advanced manufacturing.

MR Elastomer Aging & Vibration Analysis 🧲

Investigated how humidity aging changes damping in silicone-based magnetorheological elastomers (27 vol.% carbonyl iron). Free-vibration tests with LabVIEW data acquisition and logarithmic-decrement analysis showed a 15–21% increase in damping ratio and up to a 20% drop in natural frequency after 2 weeks of exposure.
